# How do I declare AI use in my submission?

If your unit requires an AI-use declaration, you complete it as part of
submitting your assignment. This takes about two minutes.

## Steps

1. Open the assignment and select **Submit**.
2. On the declaration screen, choose the statement that matches what you did:
   - *I did not use AI tools for this task.*
   - *I used AI tools as permitted for this task* — a text box will appear.
3. If a text box appears, describe your use in one or two specific sentences:
   the tool, what you used it for, and what remained your own work.
4. Select **Confirm declaration**, then complete your submission as normal.

## What to write in the text box

Be specific and brief. For example:

> I used ChatGPT to suggest an essay outline. I wrote all submitted text
> myself.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I edit my declaration after submitting?**
Before the due date, yes: reopen the submission and choose **Edit
declaration**. After the due date, contact your unit coordinator.

**What if I'm not sure whether my use was permitted?**
Check the assessment instructions in your unit outline. If they don't answer
it, ask your unit coordinator *before* submitting.

**Will declaring AI use lower my mark?**
Declared use that the task permits is not penalised. Declarations exist so
your marker understands how the work was produced.
