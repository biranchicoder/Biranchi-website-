---
title: "Thematic Analysis"
type: method
summary: "Identifying, reviewing and defining patterns of meaning across qualitative data sets."
order: 4
---

I use thematic analysis to move from coded data to defensible themes:
familiarisation, systematic coding, theme construction, review against the
full data set, and clear definition of each theme's scope and boundaries.
I document the analytic trail from raw extract to reported theme.
