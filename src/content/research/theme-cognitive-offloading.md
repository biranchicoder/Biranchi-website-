---
title: "Cognitive Offloading"
type: theme
summary: "When delegating thinking to AI supports learning, and when it hollows out the capabilities education is meant to build."
order: 4
---

Offloading routine cognitive work can free capacity for deeper thinking — or it
can remove exactly the effortful practice through which capability develops.
I study how students offload tasks to generative AI, which forms of offloading
are educationally productive, and how curriculum and assessment can direct
offloading toward growth rather than dependence.
