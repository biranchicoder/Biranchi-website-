---
title: "Scoping Review"
type: method
summary: "Mapping the breadth of an emerging field — key concepts, evidence types and gaps — where the literature is fast-moving and heterogeneous."
order: 2
---

For fast-moving topics such as generative AI in education, scoping reviews map
what kinds of evidence exist, how concepts are being defined and where the gaps
lie. I use scoping methods to chart emerging debates before committing to
narrower systematic syntheses.
