---
title: "Distributed Cognition"
type: theme
summary: "Understanding learning as cognition spread across people, tools and AI systems, and what that means for attribution of intellectual work."
order: 2
---

Cognition has never been confined to a single head: notebooks, calculators and
collaborators have always carried part of the load. Generative AI extends this
distribution dramatically. I draw on distributed-cognition theory to analyse
where thinking actually happens in AI-assisted study, and how education should
attribute and assess intellectual work that is genuinely shared with machines.
