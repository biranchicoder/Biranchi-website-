---
title: "Human–AI Co-Agency"
type: theme
summary: "Models of shared agency in which humans and AI systems jointly produce work, while humans remain accountable for meaning and judgement."
order: 3
---

Rather than framing AI use as either cheating or a neutral tool, co-agency
examines how humans and AI systems act together — who initiates, who evaluates,
who decides. My research develops vocabulary and criteria for distinguishing
legitimate co-agency, where the human directs and answers for the work, from
substitution, where human judgement quietly drops out of the loop.
