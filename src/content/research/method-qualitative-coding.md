---
title: "Qualitative Coding"
type: method
summary: "Systematic labelling of textual data with structured codebooks, supporting transparent and auditable analysis."
order: 3
---

I develop and apply structured codebooks to textual data — policy documents,
guidance materials, interview transcripts — with defined codes, decision rules
and worked examples. Coding is documented so that analytic decisions can be
audited and, where appropriate, checked by a second coder.
