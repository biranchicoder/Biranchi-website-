title: "Systematic Literature Review"
type: method
summary: "Structured, protocol-driven synthesis of research evidence with explicit search, screening and appraisal criteria."
order: 1
---

I conduct systematic reviews with pre-specified search strategies, inclusion
and exclusion criteria, screening procedures and quality appraisal, following
established reporting conventions (e.g. PRISMA-style flow). This supports
defensible claims about what the evidence base does — and does not — show.
