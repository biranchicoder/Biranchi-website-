---
title: "Evaluative Judgement"
type: theme
summary: "The capability to appraise the quality of work — one's own, others' and AI-generated — as a central goal of AI-era assessment."
order: 5
---

If AI can produce fluent work on demand, the scarce human skill becomes knowing
whether that work is any good. Evaluative judgement — the capacity to appraise
quality against standards — is central to my account of what assessment should
cultivate. I examine how verification, critique and quality appraisal of AI
output can be designed into assessment rather than left to chance.
