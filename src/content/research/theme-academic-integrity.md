---
title: "Academic Integrity"
type: theme
summary: "Rethinking authorship, honesty and fair assessment decisions when generative AI is part of ordinary study practice."
order: 6
---

Generative AI has unsettled the categories academic integrity relies on:
authorship, originality, unauthorised assistance. My work analyses how
institutions define acceptable AI use, how integrity decisions are actually
made, and how policy can stay fair to students while protecting the meaning
of qualifications. This theme drives my policy-analysis and case-portfolio
projects.
