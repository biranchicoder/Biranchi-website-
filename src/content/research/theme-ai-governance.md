title: "Responsible AI & AI Governance"
type: theme
summary: "How institutions translate AI principles into workable governance, and whether that governance produces consistent, accountable decisions as AI capability increases."
order: 0
---

Institutions are writing AI principles and policy at speed, often from very
different premises. I study how governance instruments — academic integrity
policies, assessment rules, disclosure requirements, staff guidance — regulate
AI use in practice, where they conflict, and how their real-world consistency
and accountability can be tested rather than assumed. Higher education is
where I have tested this most extensively, but the underlying governance
question applies to any institution deploying generative AI systems.
