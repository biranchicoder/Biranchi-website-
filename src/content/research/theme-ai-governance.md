Universities are writing AI policy at speed, often from very different premises.
I study how governance instruments — academic integrity policies, assessment
rules, disclosure requirements, staff guidance — regulate student and staff AI
use in practice, where they conflict, and how their real-world consistency can
be tested rather than assumed.
