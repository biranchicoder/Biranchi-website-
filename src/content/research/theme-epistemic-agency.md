---
title: "Epistemic Agency"
type: theme
summary: "How learners retain the capacity to form, justify and take responsibility for their own knowledge when AI shares the cognitive work."
order: 1
---

Epistemic agency describes a learner's active role in constructing, evaluating
and taking responsibility for knowledge. My work examines what happens to this
agency when generative AI can perform much of the intellectual labour of
studying — and what pedagogy and assessment must do to keep students the
authors of their own understanding.
